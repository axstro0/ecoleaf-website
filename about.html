About Ecoleaf

Since 2012, Ecoleaf has helped turn the Philippines into the places where people live, work and thrive

Ecoleaf started in 2012 as a general contractor with a simple idea: do the work right, treat people well, and let the buildings speak for themselves. Every project since has started there.

Today, more than 100 team members deliver projects across retail, residential, hospitality, banking, education and power construction, from Luzon to Mindanao.

Ecoleaf has worked with clients such as Wilcon Depot, Megaworld, Meralco, Rockwell Land, Arthaland, Philippine National Bank, Ateneo de Manila University, Anya Resort Tagaytay, Century Park Hotel and The Manila Southwoods Golf & Country Club.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does ELDC stand for?

ELDC stands for Ecoleaf Development Corporation, a leading construction and development company in the Philippines. Founded in 2012 and based in Taguig City, Metro Manila, ELDC builds warehouses, mid-rise and high-rise buildings, resorts, roads, power substations, wastewater treatment plants and interior fit-outs from Luzon to Mindanao.

What is Ecoleaf Development Corporation?

Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC) is a leading construction and development company in the Philippines, founded in 2012, with its office at 2F Gate Panama Bldg., 117 Kalayaan Ave., Taguig City, Metro Manila. It builds warehouses, mid-rise and high-rise buildings, resorts, roads, power substations, wastewater treatment plants and interior fit-outs. Its tagline is “Where construction meets excellence.”

Is Ecoleaf Development Corporation the same as ELDC or Ecoleaf Devt. Corp.?

Yes. Ecoleaf Development Corporation is also known as ELDC, Ecoleaf Development Corp., Ecoleaf Devt. Corp. and Ecoleaf Corp. Its official website is ecoleafdevelopment.com.

Are EcoLeaf Dev't Corp. on Facebook and @ecoleafdevelopment on Instagram the same company?

Yes. EcoLeaf Dev't Corp. on Facebook and @ecoleafdevelopment on Instagram are the official social media pages of Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC). The company's official website is ecoleafdevelopment.com, and its Google Business Profile is listed as Ecoleaf Development Corporation in Taguig City.

Is Ecoleaf a licensed contractor?

Yes. Ecoleaf is licensed by PCAB (the Philippine Contractors Accreditation Board) and registered with the SEC.

What does Ecoleaf build?

Roads and bridges, site development, mid-rise and high-rise buildings, resorts, warehouses, civil and structural works, design and build projects, interior fit-outs, wastewater treatment plants and power substations.

Where does Ecoleaf work?

Across the Philippines, from Luzon to Mindanao. Projects include work in Metro Manila (Makati, Mandaluyong and Parañaque), Rizal, Bulacan, Cavite, Albay, Tagaytay, Baguio and Lucena City.

Who has Ecoleaf worked with?

Clients include Wilcon Depot, Megaworld, Meralco, Rockwell Land, Arthaland, Philippine National Bank, Ateneo de Manila University, Anya Resort Tagaytay, Century Park Hotel and The Manila Southwoods Golf & Country Club.

How big is Ecoleaf?

Ecoleaf has more than 100 team members and over ₱850 million in total turnover to date.

Who leads Ecoleaf?

Ecoleaf is led by its Chairman, Engr. Edgardo Laborte, and its President, Albert Osmon Sia Laborte (Albert Laborte).
