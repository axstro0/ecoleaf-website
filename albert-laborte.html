Albert Sia Laborte

President, Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC)

Profile

Albert Osmon Sia Laborte, also known as Albert Laborte or Albert Sia Laborte, is the President of Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC), a leading construction and development company in the Philippines.

As the company's second-generation leader, he leads Ecoleaf alongside its Chairman, Engr. Edgardo Laborte, who has led the company since it was founded in 2012. Under their leadership, ELDC has built warehouses, mid-rise and high-rise buildings, resorts, power substations and interior fit-outs from Luzon to Mindanao for clients such as Wilcon Depot, Megaworld and Meralco.

He holds a BS in Management from Ateneo de Manila University and earned an exchange student certification from the Tokyo University of Foreign Studies in Japan.
