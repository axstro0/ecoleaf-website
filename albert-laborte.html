Albert Sia Laborte

President, Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC)

Profile

Albert Osmon Sia Laborte, also known as Albert Laborte or Albert Sia Laborte, is the President of Ecoleaf Development Corporation (ELDC), a leading construction and development company in the Philippines.

Founded in 2012 and based in Taguig City, Metro Manila, ELDC is a PCAB-licensed general contractor that has grown to more than 100 team members and over ₱850 million in total turnover, building warehouses, mid-rise and high-rise buildings, resorts, roads, power substations, wastewater treatment plants and interior fit-outs from Luzon to Mindanao.

Leading Ecoleaf

As the company's second-generation leader, Albert leads ELDC alongside its Chairman, Engr. Edgardo Laborte. Under their leadership, Ecoleaf has delivered projects for clients such as Wilcon Depot, Megaworld, Meralco, Rockwell Land, Arthaland, Philippine National Bank and Ateneo de Manila University, including Wilcon Depot warehouses in Daraga and Antipolo, Meralco substation works and the renovation of the Ateneo Professional School in Rockwell. His approach is simple: great buildings begin with great relationships, and every project should end as a win for both sides.

Education and honors

Albert studied at La Salle Green Hills from elementary through high school, earning multiple academic awards and ranking 4th in a class of more than 350 students. He went on to Ateneo de Manila University, where he was a Dean's Lister and earned a BS in Management, and he completed an exchange program at the Tokyo University of Foreign Studies in Japan.
