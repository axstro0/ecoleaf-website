The Team

Meet the leaders

Engr. Edgardo
Laborte

Chairman

Engr. Edgardo Laborte is the Chairman of Ecoleaf Development Corporation. A licensed engineer, he has led the company since it was founded in 2012.

He remains driven to grow Ecoleaf while staying true to its tagline: where construction meets excellence.

Albert Sia
Laborte

President

Albert Osmon Sia Laborte leads Ecoleaf Development Corporation as its President. As the company's second-generation leader, he continues to lead Ecoleaf as the nation's top builder.

He holds a BS in Management from Ateneo de Manila University and earned an exchange student certification from the Tokyo University of Foreign Studies in Japan.
